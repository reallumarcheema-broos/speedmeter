Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This policy explains what {{SITE_NAME}} collects when you use the speed test, what it does not collect, and the choices you have. It applies to {{SITE_URL}}.

The short version

  • Your speed test results are stored in your browser only. We do not upload or keep them.
  • The test transfers random, meaningless data. It never reads files from your device.
  • We show advertising from Google AdSense, which uses cookies. You can refuse them.
  • We do not sell personal information.

What the speed test does

Measuring a connection means moving data across it. During a test your browser downloads a stream of randomly generated bytes from our server and uploads a similar stream back. That data is meaningless filler, generated fresh by the server. It is never written to disk, never inspected, and discarded the moment it arrives.

Your results — download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter — are calculated by the JavaScript running in your browser. The server never receives them.

Information we collect

Server logs

Like virtually every web server, ours processes the information your browser sends with each request: your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser's user agent string and referring page. This is used to operate the service, diagnose faults and detect abuse such as automated hammering of the test endpoints.

Your IP address is also shown back to you on the results screen, because knowing which connection you tested is useful. It is displayed, not stored.

Stored in your browser

The site uses your browser's local storage — not cookies — for three things:

  • Test history: your last ten results, so you can compare them over time.
  • Theme preference: whether you chose light or dark.
  • Consent choice: your answer to the cookie banner, so you are not asked repeatedly.

This data never leaves your device and is not readable by us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it. You can also clear your test history at any time with the Clear button under Recent tests.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store names, email addresses, accounts or payment details. There is nothing to sign up for.

Your consent choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept advertising cookies. Until you accept, we signal to Google that advertising storage and personalisation are denied, and you will see non-personalised ads instead. You can change your answer at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page.
